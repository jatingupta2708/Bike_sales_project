Graph 1: Total Listings & Key Metrics (KPI Cards)
Business Question: What are the total listings, average price, average resale price, and average mileage?

Objective: Provide high-level summary of the dataset.

Best Plotly Chart: KPI Cards (numeric values)

Aggregation: Count, Mean(Price), Mean(Resale Price), Mean(Mileage)

GroupBy Needed: No

Columns Used: Price, Resale Price, Mileage
